Terms of Service

Scope

Aurelius Fire Risk provides fire risk assessment and related fire safety services in accordance with the agreed quotation and scope.

Assessment limitations

Unless expressly agreed otherwise, assessments are Type 1, non-intrusive assessments based on visual inspection of reasonably accessible areas.

Concealed voids, hidden compartmentation, concealed construction, cladding systems, destructive investigation, DSEAR assessments, emergency lighting lux testing, alarm audibility testing and property or business continuity risks are outside the standard scope unless specifically agreed.

Responsible person

The Responsible Person retains their statutory duties for fire safety and must act on significant findings and other matters identified by the assessment.

Access and information

The client must provide reasonable access to relevant areas and provide information and documents reasonably required for the agreed service.

Reports

The final assessment report records observations and recommendations within the agreed scope. It does not transfer the client's legal responsibilities.

Payment

Payment terms are those stated on the accepted quotation or invoice. Where online payment is used, payment is processed through the nominated payment provider.

Cancellation and rescheduling

Bookings may be rescheduled subject to availability. Any applicable cancellation terms will be stated in the engagement documentation.
