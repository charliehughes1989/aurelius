Privacy Policy

Aurelius Fire Risk respects your privacy and handles personal information only where necessary to provide fire risk assessment and related services.

Information we may collect

This may include your name, contact details, business details, premises information, booking information, documents and information required to arrange or complete services.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, arrange visits, manage client accounts, provide reports and documents, process payments and administer our services.

Documents

Documents uploaded through the client portal may include fire safety and premises information. Access should be limited to authorised users and information should only be provided where appropriate.

Payments

Online card payments may be processed by third-party payment providers. Payment card details are not intended to be stored directly by Aurelius Fire Risk.

Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably required for business, contractual, legal, accounting or regulatory purposes.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction and objection to processing.

Contact

For privacy enquiries, please use the contact/enquiry facility provided on this website.
